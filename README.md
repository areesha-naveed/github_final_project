Simple Interest Calculator

Description

The Simple Interest Calculator is a simple application that calculates the simple interest based on the principal amount, interest rate, and time period provided by the user.

It is designed to make simple interest calculations quick and easy.

Formula

The calculator uses the following formula:

Simple Interest (SI) = (P × R × T) / 100

Where:

- P = Principal amount
- R = Rate of interest per year (%)
- T = Time period in years
- SI = Simple Interest

The total amount is calculated as:

Total Amount = Principal + Simple Interest

Inputs

The calculator requires three inputs:

1. Principal Amount (P) – The initial amount of money.
2. Rate of Interest (R) – The annual interest rate in percentage.
3. Time (T) – The period for which the money is invested or borrowed, in years.

Outputs

The calculator provides:

- Simple Interest – The interest calculated using the simple interest formula.
- Total Amount – The principal amount plus the calculated simple interest.

Example

Suppose the user enters:

- Principal = 10,000
- Rate = 5%
- Time = 2 years

Using the formula:

SI = (10,000 × 5 × 2) / 100

SI = 1,000

Therefore:

Total Amount = 10,000 + 1,000 = 11,000

Expected Output

- Simple Interest: 1,000
- Total Amount: 11,000

How It Works

1. The user enters the principal amount.
2. The user enters the annual interest rate.
3. The user enters the time period in years.
4. The calculator applies the simple interest formula.
5. The calculated simple interest and total amount are displayed to the user.

Purpose

The purpose of this calculator is to provide a simple and convenient way to calculate simple interest and understand how the principal amount, interest rate, and time period affect the final amount.
